# Support

For help with Together Ledger, write to ledger-support@together-ledger.com. Tell us whether you use the iPhone app, the Android app or the website, and what you were trying to do. Never send your password.

## Signing in

If you've forgotten your password, choose I forgot my password on the sign-in screen. We email a link to the address on your account. The link works once and lasts 30 minutes.

## Purchases

A journey of two is free. If you bought room for more people in the App Store or Google Play, choose Restore purchases in the app's Settings to bring it back on a new phone.

A refund for a purchase made in the App Store or Google Play comes from Apple or Google, under their own rules. For a payment made on our website, write to ledger-support@together-ledger.com, and anything we charged in error is refunded in full.

## Deleting your account

In the phone app, open Settings, then Delete account. On the website, open Account settings, then Delete account. You confirm with your password, or, for an account opened with Apple or Google, just by typing DELETE.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought in the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it in your Apple account's subscriptions, or in Google Play's Payments and subscriptions, or it keeps renewing.

What is deleted, and what stays with a shared journey, is set out in the [privacy policy](https://app.together-ledger.com/privacy).

## Your privacy and your data

For a question about your privacy, or a request about your data, write to legal@together-ledger.com. The [terms of use](https://app.together-ledger.com/terms) say what each side can expect.
