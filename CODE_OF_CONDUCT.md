# Code of conduct

Together Ledger welcomes people of every relationship structure, gender, orientation, culture, income, ability, and technical background.

Contributors are expected to be respectful, specific, and curious. Harassment, demeaning language, coercive relationship advice, financial shaming, doxxing, or publication of another person's private data is not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

If something happens, contact the repository owner privately rather than escalating a sensitive situation in a public thread.
