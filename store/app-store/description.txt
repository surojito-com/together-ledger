Together Ledger is a private place for two people to hold what matters: the moments, plans and memories you want to come back to, without the noise of a feed.

Hold a moment
Write down what happened while it's still fresh: a promise, a memory, a heart-to-heart, a boundary, a repair request, or a kind of moment you name yourself. Give it a date, a place in your own words, an amount of money if it matters, and as much detail as you want. You can edit or delete it later. You can hold a moment without a connection; it's sent when you're back online.

Choose who sees it
Every moment carries its visibility, shown in a shape, a word and a border:
- Private stays with you.
- Share later waits until you deliberately share it.
- Shared now opens it to everyone in the journey.
Sharing asks you first, and a shared moment can't be made private again, so nothing is quietly taken back.

Return to it together
Start a conversation about something you want to come back to, and mark it resolved when you have. The journey keeps a history that is only ever added to: what changed, who changed it, and when.

Grow when you choose
A journey of two is free. You can make room for more people whenever you're ready, and someone new joins only when everyone already in the journey agrees.

Room for more people
The owner of a journey can make room for up to 51 or up to 101 people. Their first paid journey renews every month through the App Store; any other journey can have room for a week or a month at a time. Room belongs to the journey, so it's the same on every device anyone in it uses. If room stops being paid for, the people beyond what's covered rest: they can still read the whole journey, and no history is lost.

Private by design
- No advertising and no analytics trackers. We don't sell your information.
- No scores and no feed. Money can be noted as context, never counted as a score.
- Delete your account in the app at any time.

On your phone and on the web
Sign in with the same account and your journeys are there.

Make it yours
Choose Light, Dark, Green or Flexoki. Your theme changes only your own view.

Together Ledger is for adults aged 18 and over. It isn't therapy, financial advice or a substitute for professional support.

A monthly subscription renews automatically until you cancel it in your App Store subscriptions, at least 24 hours before it renews. Deleting your account doesn't cancel it.
