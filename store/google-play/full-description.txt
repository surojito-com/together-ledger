Together Ledger is a private place for two people to hold what matters: the moments, plans and memories you want to come back to, without the noise of a feed.

Hold a moment
Write down what happened while it's still fresh: a promise, a memory, a heart-to-heart, a boundary, a repair request, a practical matter, or a kind of moment you name yourself. Give it a date, a place in your own words, a photo if you like, and as much detail as you want. You can edit or delete it later. You can hold a moment without a connection; it's sent when you're back online.

Choose who sees it
Every moment carries its visibility, shown in a shape, a word and a border:
- Private stays with you.
- Share later waits until you deliberately share it.
- Shared now opens it to everyone in the journey.
A shared moment can't be made private again, so nothing is quietly taken back.

Check in gently
A short check-in asks one question at a time. Nothing you write in it is saved.

Return to it together
The ledger shows your recent moments and what wants care. Start a return-to conversation about a moment and come back to it when you're ready. Milestones mark what you've done together, never how well you're doing. The journey keeps a history that is only ever added to: what changed, who changed it, and when.

Grow when you choose
A journey of two is free. You can add more people whenever you're ready, and someone new joins only when everyone already in the journey agrees.

Private by design
- No advertising and no analytics trackers. We don't sell your information.
- No scores and no feed. Money can be noted as context, never counted as a score.
- Export all your journeys whenever you want a copy.
- Delete your account at any time.

On your phone and on the web
Sign in with the same account and your journeys are there.

Make it yours
Choose Light, Dark, Green or Flexoki. Your theme changes only your own view.

Together Ledger is for adults aged 18 and over. It isn't therapy, financial advice or a substitute for professional support.
