Gelasio, Lora, Source Serif 4, Noto Serif and Charis SIL are embedded as subsets covering only these specimen characters — a few KB each, which says nothing about a shipped bundle size. All are SIL OFL 1.1, which permits bundling in a commercial app. Georgia is proprietary and cannot be embedded, which is why this page has to ask your device for it.
